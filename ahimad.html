Our Services

Woreda Administration Office

Woreda Administration Office: This office serves as the highest executive body within the district, responsible for overall governance, leadership, and policy enforcement, ensuring that all regional and federal policies and programs are effectively coordinated and implemented across every sector, while also acting as the central hub for citizen communication, resource allocation, and general administrative oversight to ensure cohesive public service delivery and the realization of local development goals.

Finance and Economic Development

The core mandate of this department is to manage the district's public finance and guide its economic direction through activities that include preparing the annual Woreda budget, overseeing revenue collection from local taxes and fees, allocating funds to sectoral offices, monitoring the financial performance of development projects, and leading the strategic economic planning process to foster sustainable local growth and wealth creation.

Agriculture and Natural Resources:

This office is tasked with enhancing the livelihoods of rural communities by focusing on food security and environmental protection, primarily by delivering crucial agricultural extension services and technical training to farmers, coordinating the supply and distribution of essential farm inputs like improved seeds and fertilizer, promoting the development of small-scale irrigation schemes, and regulating and managing the sustainable use of natural resources, including land, forests, and soil conservation efforts.

Health Office

The Health Office is the primary authority for ensuring the well-being of the Woreda population by planning, coordinating, and delivering essential primary healthcare services, managing the network of health centers and health posts, running disease prevention campaigns and health education programs, and coordinating emergency public health responses, while also ensuring the availability of necessary medical supplies and supportive supervision for frontline health workers.

Education Office

This department holds the responsibility for managing and improving the quality of education across the Woreda, which involves overseeing the administration and operations of all government primary and secondary schools, ensuring the implementation of the national and regional curriculum, managing teacher deployment and professional development, and coordinating initiatives to increase school enrollment, retention, and educational outcomes for all students.

Water Resources and Energy

Dedicated to improving public access to basic infrastructure, this office focuses on developing, maintaining, and managing water supply systems for both rural and urban areas, ensuring the quality and availability of drinking water, and working on expanding access to various forms of energy, including coordinating the implementation of electrification and alternative energy projects within the district.

Justice/Court

This sector maintains law and order within the Woreda by encompassing both the local judicial and law enforcement functions; the Police are responsible for crime prevention, investigation, and public safety, while the local Woreda Court handles the administration of justice through processing local civil and criminal cases, resolving disputes, and executing the legal proceedings required to uphold the rule of law

Women, Children, and Youth Affairs

The core mission of this office is to promote social equity and protect the rights of vulnerable groups by designing and implementing programs that aim to empower women socially and economically, advocating for gender equality, ensuring the protection and welfare of children, and coordinating various capacity-building, job-seeking, and recreational initiatives aimed at engaging and developing the youth population.

Labor and Social Affairs

This office focuses on social safety and economic opportunity by managing and registering local employment services to facilitate job matching, administering social support and safety net programs for the neediest segments of the population, regulating and promoting sound industrial relations in local workplaces, and ensuring compliance with labor laws and occupational safety standards across the district.

Urban Development and Housing

Where applicable, this office is responsible for the systematic and orderly growth of towns and municipal centers within the Woreda, primarily through drafting and enforcing local urban land-use plans, regulating and issuing construction permits for private and public buildings, coordinating the provision of municipal services, and leading initiatives for developing affordable public housing and urban infrastructure.

Cooperative Development

This department is specifically tasked with supporting the formation and sustainability of various local self-help groups, most notably agricultural producer and service cooperatives, by providing registration services, offering management training, facilitating access to markets and credit, and ensuring that these community-owned enterprises operate effectively to improve the collective economic bargaining power and welfare of their members.

Public Service and Human Resource Development

This office is central to ensuring a competent and effective civil service by focusing on the administration of government employees across all Woreda departments, which includes managing recruitment, performance appraisal, compensation, and promotion systems, developing and conducting relevant training and skill-upgrading programs, and working continuously to improve the quality, efficiency, and ethical standards of public service delivery.

Our Vision

The vision of Chewaka Woreda office is to become a model district in the Oromia Regional State by 2035, distinguished by a secure, cohesive, and resilient community, fundamentally supported by the digitalization of its governance and the sustainable, data-driven management of its rich natural environment. This vision will be achieved by establishing a high-trust, low-crime social environment and proactive security measures; digitalizing core public services to enhance transparency and citizen access; utilizing Geographic Information System (GIS) and modern monitoring tools to halt land degradation and enforce informed land use planning; and reinforcing the Woreda's foundational mission by leveraging digital market information systems to ensure household food self-sufficiency and promote the diversification of livelihoods away from subsistence agriculture.

About Us

Based on its establishment and the massive government-sponsored resettlement operation beginning in the early 2000s, the Chewaka Wereda Office has played a central role in providing essential public services to the relocated population up to the present day. These services, which were largely unavailable before the resettlement, include the establishment of schools, health facilities, water supply centers, and all-weather roads, ensuring that basic social amenities were made accessible at the Kebele (village) level across the Wereda. Furthermore, the office has been instrumental in the administration of the resettlement scheme, which involved the allocation of landholdings and coordination with various government departments to support agricultural production and livelihood improvements for the thousands of settlers moving from drought-prone areas of East and West Hararghe.
